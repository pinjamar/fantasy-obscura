Style guide for all banners:
- Photorealistic cinematic fantasy digital art
- Dramatic atmospheric lighting, strong contrast between dark shadows and vibrant focal light
- No text, no legible writing anywhere in image
- No clearly visible character faces (implied presence, silhouettes, or backs only)
- Square crop (1:1), approximately 800×800px output
- Mood-first: each image should feel like the series before you know what it is
