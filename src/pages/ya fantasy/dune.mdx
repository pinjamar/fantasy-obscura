# Dune Reading Order

Frank Herbert's epic science fiction saga spans decades and multiple worlds.

## Original Series by Frank Herbert

1. Dune (1965)
2. Dune Messiah (1969)
3. Children of Dune (1976)
4. God Emperor of Dune (1981)
5. Heretics of Dune (1984)
6. Chapterhouse: Dune (1985)

## Extended Universe by Brian Herbert & Kevin J. Anderson

The original six books are considered essential. The extended universe continues the story but with a different tone.
